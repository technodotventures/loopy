the mark

Two laps, one line.

The loop is two o's drawn as one pen line: a small one, then a bigger one, crossing over and under in the middle. It reads as "oo" inside the name and as a loop on its own, and it says the whole idea in one stroke: every lap ends a little ahead of the last.

Each lap a little bigger

The second o is larger than the first, both sitting on the baseline. The next lap always comes out ahead of the last.

Over, then under

At the crossing the new lap passes over the old one, the way a pen line does. It's what makes the loop drawn rather than set.

Amber means kept

The loop is the only amber in the logo, the same amber that marks a kept result on every chart.

wordmark

The loop is the oo.

The letters are plotter strokes: one pen, one weight, round ends, every letter on the same monospaced grid. All of it is vector, so the wordmark renders anywhere without a font, a GitHub README included.

icon

The loop on its own.

Out of the wordmark, the loop is the icon: amber on an Ink tile for apps and avatars, Ink on paper where colour isn't available.

App icon, avatar and favicon at 96, 48, 28 and 16 px. The stroke thickens as the size drops.
